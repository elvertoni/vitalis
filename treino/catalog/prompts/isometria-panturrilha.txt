EXERCISE: Isometric calf raise hold (Isometria de panturrilha). EQUIPMENT: flat floor and a wall for hand support.
CAMERA: strict side view, wall on the right.
This is an isometric hold: LEFT PANEL shows the setup, RIGHT PANEL shows the hold. Use a
single small arrow in the left panel showing how the hold is reached, and no arrow
in the right panel.
LEFT PANEL — SETUP: standing tall facing the wall, hands lightly on the wall at chest height, feet flat hip-width, body upright.
RIGHT PANEL — HOLD: same position, raised onto the balls of the feet and holding there, heels high, body upright and not leaning on the wall.
MUST BE VISIBLE: body upright in both panels; heels clearly raised in the hold; hands only lightly on the wall.
AVOID: text, logos, extra people, extra limbs, deformed hands or fingers, rounded lower back, leaning the body onto the wall, bent knees.

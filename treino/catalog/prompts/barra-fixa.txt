EXERCISE: Pull-up (Barra fixa). EQUIPMENT: fixed pull-up bar.
CAMERA: front view in BOTH panels: the character faces the viewer, the bar runs left to right
across the panel, and both hands are seen on the bar. Identical camera in the left and right panels.
LEFT PANEL — START: hanging from the bar with an overhand grip a bit wider than the shoulders, arms fully extended, legs straight or slightly bent together, body still, core braced.
RIGHT PANEL — END: body pulled up until the chin is above the bar, elbows pointing down, chest close to the bar, legs in the same position.
MUST BE VISIBLE: arms fully extended at the start; chin above the bar at the end; body not swinging.
AVOID: text, logos, extra people, extra limbs, deformed hands or fingers, rounded lower back, kipping or swinging, half range of motion, feet touching the floor.

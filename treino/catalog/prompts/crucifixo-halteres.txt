EXERCISE: Dumbbell fly (Crucifixo com halteres). EQUIPMENT: two dumbbells and a flat bench.
CAMERA: 3/4 view from the head end and slightly above, so both arms are visible.
LEFT PANEL — START: lying on the back on the flat bench, feet flat on the floor, arms open wide in an arc, dumbbells at about chest level (not below the torso line), elbows slightly bent, palms facing up toward the ceiling.
RIGHT PANEL — END: same body position, arms closed in the same arc, dumbbells together above the chest, same slight elbow bend, palms facing each other.
MUST BE VISIBLE: same slight elbow bend in both panels; dumbbells not lower than the torso at the start; buttocks on the bench.
AVOID: text, logos, extra people, extra limbs, deformed hands or fingers, rounded lower back, fully straight elbows, arms dropping far below the bench, turning it into a press.

- Plain flat background color #F5F4F0, edge to edge. No scenery, floor line only if needed

- Same character in every image: adult, medium athletic build, gender-neutral appearance,

- Figure in neutral greys (#5C5C5A body tones, #1A1A1A outlines). Single accent color
  #5D674F, used ONLY for the motion arrows.
- Two side-by-side panels of equal size, separated by a thin vertical gap: LEFT = start
  position, RIGHT = end position. Same camera angle, scale and equipment in both.
- Camera: strict side view, or 3/4 view only when the side view hides the key joint

- One or two thin curved arrows per panel showing the direction of movement.
- No text, letters, numbers, labels or watermarks anywhere. No muscle highlighting (the

- Equipment drawn accurately and to scale (barbell with plates, dumbbells, flat or incline
  bench, cable machine, pull-up bar).

EXERCISE: Cable Pallof press (Pallof press na polia). EQUIPMENT: cable tower with the pulley
set at chest height and a single handle.
CAMERA: front view, the character facing the viewer; the cable tower stands at the LEFT edge of
each panel and the cable runs horizontally from the tower to the character's hands.
LEFT PANEL — START: standing side-on to the tower (the tower is at the character's right side),
feet shoulder-width, knees slightly bent, both hands holding the handle against the middle of the
chest, cable taut and horizontal.
RIGHT PANEL — END: same stance, both arms pressed straight out TOWARD THE VIEWER, hands still in
front of the middle of the chest (the arms are seen foreshortened, pointing at the camera), cable
taut and horizontal from the tower to the hands, torso square to the viewer, not turned toward the
tower.
ARROWS: none in either panel (exception to the arrow rule above: the press goes straight at
the camera, and any arrow would read as a sideways or downward move).
MUST BE VISIBLE: cable perpendicular to the arms in the right panel; torso square to the viewer in
both panels; hands centred in front of the chest in both panels.
AVOID: text, logos, extra people, extra limbs, deformed hands or fingers, rounded lower back, arms
pointing sideways, torso rotated toward the tower, a different face or hairstyle from the
reference, any arrow.

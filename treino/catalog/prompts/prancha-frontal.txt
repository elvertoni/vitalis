EXERCISE: Front plank (Prancha frontal). EQUIPMENT: thin exercise mat on the floor.
CAMERA: strict side view, head on the left.
This is an isometric hold: LEFT PANEL shows the setup, RIGHT PANEL shows the hold. Use a
single small arrow in the left panel showing the knees lifting off the mat, and no arrow
in the right panel.
LEFT PANEL — SETUP: kneeling on the mat, forearms flat on the mat, elbows directly under
the shoulders, forearms parallel, hands relaxed, neck neutral, eyes looking at the floor.
RIGHT PANEL — HOLD: knees lifted, legs straight, weight on the forearms and the balls of
the feet, body forming one straight line from head through shoulders, hips and knees to
the heels, elbows directly under the shoulders.
MUST BE VISIBLE: elbows under the shoulders in both panels; straight line from head to
heels in the hold; hips level with the shoulders, not sagging and not raised.

back, hips sagging toward the floor, hips piked up high, head hanging down.

EXERCISE: Bent-over barbell row (Remada curvada com barra). EQUIPMENT: Olympic barbell
with plates.
CAMERA: 3/4 view from front-left and slightly above, so the flat back and the bar under the
torso are both visible.
LEFT PANEL — START: feet hip-width, knees slightly bent, hips pushed far back, torso hinged forward
until it is almost parallel to the floor (about 20 degrees above horizontal). The whole back, from hips to the base of the neck,
is ONE STRAIGHT FLAT LINE like a tabletop, chest up, neck in line with the spine. Arms hang
straight down from the shoulders holding the barbell with an overhand grip; the bar hangs just
below the knees.
RIGHT PANEL — END: exactly the same legs, hips and flat torso angle as the left panel. Only the
arms move: elbows driven up and back past the torso, the barbell pulled straight up until it
touches the belly at the navel; because the torso is almost horizontal, the bar is far above the
knees and clearly away from the thighs, and the elbows rise above the line of the back. Back still one straight
flat line.
MUST BE VISIBLE: tabletop-flat back in both panels with no hump between the shoulder blades; same
torso angle in both panels; bar touching the belly at the navel in the right panel.
AVOID: text, logos, extra people, extra limbs, deformed hands or fingers, rounded lower back,
hunched upper back, torso rising, bar resting on the thighs or hips.

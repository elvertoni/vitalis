CAMERA: strict side view.
LEFT PANEL — START: hips on the pad, ankles locked under the foot rollers, arms crossed on the chest, torso hinged down at the hips toward the floor, spine neutral.
RIGHT PANEL — END: torso raised until the body forms one straight line from heels to head along the bench angle, arms still crossed.
MUST BE VISIBLE: top position is a straight line, not arched beyond it; neutral spine in both panels; pad under the hips, not the belly.

EXERCISE: Seated dumbbell shoulder press (Desenvolvimento com halteres). EQUIPMENT: two
dumbbells and an upright bench with a vertical backrest.
CAMERA: 3/4 view from front-left, so both arms and the backrest are visible.
LEFT PANEL — START: seated upright, back and head against the backrest, feet flat on the
floor, knees bent about 90 degrees, one dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, palms
facing forward, forearms vertical, elbows below the wrists and slightly in front of the
torso.
RIGHT PANEL — END: same seated position, both arms extended overhead with elbows almost
straight but not locked, dumbbells above the shoulders and close to each other, palms
facing forward, back still against the backrest.
MUST BE VISIBLE: back against the backrest in both panels; forearms vertical; dumbbells
at shoulder height at the start and directly above the shoulders at the end; feet on the
floor.
AVOID: text, logos, extra people, extra limbs, deformed hands or fingers, rounded lower
back, excessive arching of the lower back, dumbbells drifting forward or behind the head.
